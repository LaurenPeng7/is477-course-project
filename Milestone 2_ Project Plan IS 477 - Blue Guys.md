**Overview**  
The goal of our project is to investigate the relationship between food price inflation, agricultural food-system employment, and greenhouse gas emissions in the USA over time. We plan to combine three datasets, clean and merge them using shared year identifiers, and use visualizations and correlation analysis to identify patterns and potential relationships between these factors.

**Research Question:** “How are changes in food price inflation and agrifood-system employment associated with greenhouse gas emissions from agrifood systems in the United States over time?”

## **Constraints:**

**Data Availability:** The three datasets may have different year ranges and missing values, limiting the number of years available for analysis.

**Data Consistency:** The Consumer Price Indices dataset contains monthly observations, while the employment and emissions datasets contain annual observations. Monthly data will need to be converted to annual measurements.

**Data Quality:** Some observations may be estimated rather than directly measured, potentially affecting the accuracy of the analysis.

**Research Limitations:** Correlation analysis can identify relationships between variables but cannot establish causation.

**Dataset:**  
[**Consumer Price Indices**](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/dataset/FAO_CP?country=USA)**:** This is a dataset that tracks food-specific consumer price indices of various countries. Data that was not recorded is filled in with calculated estimates. The data is recorded monthly.  
[**Employment in Agrifood System**](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/dataset/WB_AFS?country=USA)**:** This is a two-part dataset. It includes “on-farm” data, which includes the employment data of the agricultural, forestry, and fishing sectors. It also includes “off-farm” data, which includes the employment data of food processing, food services, and transportation sectors. This is an annually updated dataset.  
[**Emissions Totals**](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/dataset/FAO_EMSTOT?country=USA)**:** This is a dataset focused on the emissions types and totals of various countries. It essentially summarizes greenhouse gas emissions calculated using IPCC Tier 1 inventory methods. It is annual data, broken down by type of gas as well as the sector.
